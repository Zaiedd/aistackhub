You finish a client call, open the recording, and realise you still have to write the summary yourself. That is the exact problem these three tools claim to solve. I tested Fathom, Fireflies.ai and Otter.ai on the same set of discovery calls, project check-ins and internal stand-ups in September 2026. Same audio, same platforms (Zoom and Google Meet), same questions: how good is the free plan, how usable is the summary, and how much does the tool get in the way.

This is not a feature checklist. It is a practical comparison for freelancers and small teams who live in meetings but do not want another $30 seat.

## Free tier reality check

**Fathom** still wins on paper. Unlimited recordings and transcriptions, instant AI summaries, clips and search. The practical limit in 2026 is the number of advanced AI summaries (around five per month on free). After that you still get the full recording and basic transcript.

**Fireflies** gives unlimited transcription but caps storage (roughly 400 minutes for the team on free) and limits AI credits. Useful for testing, not for a full week of client work.

**Otter** free is the most restricted of the three: 300 minutes per month and a hard 30-minute cap per conversation. Most people blow through it in a few longer calls.

If the decision is “I want zero cost for as long as possible,” Fathom is the clear leader.

## Summary quality and action items

I fed the same 35–40 minute discovery call into all three. Fathom produced the shortest, most readable summary with clean action items. Fireflies was slightly denser and better at pulling speaker-specific points when the conversation got messy. Otter’s live transcript is strong, but the post-call summary felt more generic in my tests.

None of them are perfect. All three still need a quick human pass before you send the follow-up to a client. The gap is how much editing that pass requires. Fathom usually needed the least.

## Bot presence and client perception

This matters more than most reviews admit. Fireflies and Otter join as visible bots by default. Some clients ask “who is that?” Others do not care. Fathom offers a bot-free option on supported setups (still in beta on some platforms in late 2026). For high-stakes client work, the ability to keep the bot invisible is a real advantage.

## CRM and team features

Fireflies is the strongest once you need notes pushed into HubSpot or Salesforce. The Pro and Business tiers make this almost automatic. Fathom adds proper CRM field sync only on the higher Business plan. Otter has integrations but they are less automatic for most small-team workflows.

If you are a solo freelancer with no CRM, this section does not matter. If you are a two-person sales or consulting team, Fireflies starts to pull ahead.

## Pricing that actually shows up on the invoice (September 2026)

| Tool | Free plan | Entry paid (annual) | Notes |
|------|-----------|---------------------|-------|
| Fathom | Unlimited recordings + limited advanced summaries | Premium ~$16/user | Team plans lower per seat with 2-user minimum |
| Fireflies | Unlimited transcription, limited storage | Pro ~$10/seat | Business ~$19/seat for heavier use |
| Otter | 300 min/mo, 30-min cap | Pro ~$8.33/user | Business higher for unlimited meetings |

Annual billing cuts the monthly rate significantly on all three. Month-to-month is roughly 40–100 % more expensive depending on the tool.

## Who should pick what

- Solo freelancer or consultant who just wants notes after the call → **Fathom free**
- Small team that already uses HubSpot/Salesforce and has more than a handful of calls per week → **Fireflies Pro**
- Someone who needs live captions during long workshops or interviews → **Otter Pro**

There is no universal winner. The right tool is the one that matches the volume of meetings and the importance of CRM versus pure note quality.
